How to use this guide

Pick a chapter from the sidebar to read its summary, key concepts, and full interview Q&A — every answer is written the way you would actually speak it in a panel. Use the Cards tab for spaced-repetition review on the bus or between meetings. Use Quiz to test yourself on tricky multiple-choice items with explanations. The Search bar at the top of the sidebar finds anything across all chapters in real time.

Priority dots tell you what to study first: red is high-frequency interview territory, gold is supporting material, grey is depth-of-knowledge.

One-week interview prep order

Day 1–2: Appendix C (pipelining) → Chapter 3 (ILP). The OoO foundations that anchor every digital design interview.
Day 3: Appendix B → Chapter 2 (memory hierarchy). Caches are the most-asked topic, period.
Day 4: Chapter 5 (multicore, coherence, consistency). Increasingly common in modern interviews.
Day 5: Chapter 1 (performance, power, Amdahl). The framework you cite when explaining design choices.
Day 6–7: Chapters 4 (DLP/GPU) and 6 (WSC) for breadth. Quiz mode + flashcards on weak areas.
